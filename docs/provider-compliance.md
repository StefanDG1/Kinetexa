# Direct provider gates

Reviewed 6 September 2026. No direct connector is enabled. File uploads and Strava bulk ZIP migration remain available. `providers:catalog` exposes each gate and unverified capability explicitly; `providers:connect` rejects connections server-side. There is no stored provider authorization, backfill worker or successful sync claim.

The adapter contract in `packages/core/providers.ts` covers authorization, exchange, refresh, revocation, profile, backfill, activities, detail, raw files, health, webhooks, reconciliation and quota state. Optional methods must agree with advertised capabilities. An enabled adapter still needs a verified implementation, private credential lifecycle, resumable jobs and hosted acceptance evidence. Contract types alone do not complete PROV-001 or PROV-003 through PROV-007.

Polar's [AccessLink documentation](https://www.polar.com/accesslink-api/) exposes raw FIT and signed webhooks but limits exercise retrieval to uploads from the preceding 30 days and after client registration. It does not provide full pre-connection exercise backfill. This is a technical observation, not approval to enable the API.

Before enabling a provider, replace its pending record with a dated approved compliance note covering allowed use, retention, display, revocation/deletion, branding, commercial terms and AI restrictions. Verify credential isolation, signed webhooks, duplicate delivery, resumable allowed history, rate limits, missed-event reconciliation and deletion. Credentials alone cannot override a gate.
