# Direct provider gates

Reviewed 6 September 2026. No direct connector is enabled. File uploads and Strava bulk ZIP migration remain available. `providers:catalog` exposes each gate and unverified capability explicitly; `providers:connect` rejects connections server-side. There is no stored provider authorization, backfill worker or successful sync claim.

The adapter contract in `packages/core/providers.ts` covers authorization, exchange, refresh, revocation, profile, backfill, activities, detail, raw files, health, webhooks, reconciliation and quota state. Optional methods must agree with advertised capabilities. An enabled adapter still needs a verified implementation, private credential lifecycle, resumable jobs and hosted acceptance evidence. Contract types alone do not complete PROV-001 or PROV-003 through PROV-007.

Polar's [AccessLink documentation](https://www.polar.com/accesslink-api/) exposes raw FIT and signed webhooks but limits exercise retrieval to uploads from the preceding 30 days and after client registration. It does not provide full pre-connection exercise backfill. This is a technical observation, not approval to enable the API.

Before enabling a provider, replace its pending record with a dated approved compliance note covering allowed use, retention, display, revocation/deletion, branding, commercial terms and AI restrictions. Verify credential isolation, signed webhooks, duplicate delivery, resumable allowed history, rate limits, missed-event reconciliation and deletion. Credentials alone cannot override a gate.

## Wahoo review, 6 September 2026

The [Cloud API portal](https://developers.wahooligan.com/cloud) requires approval and separate sandbox/production applications. Its [API agreement](https://eu.wahoofitness.com/wahoo-api-agreement) restricts competing services, caching geographic/user information and charging for API-related functionality. It also requires deletion on user request or subscription cancellation, removal within 48 hours when Wahoo requests it, and prompt incident notification. These provisions conflict with the intended retained canonical dataset and hosted analytics unless permission or a compatible agreement resolves them. Access is currently free but can become paid. Attribution, links to the athlete's Wahoo account, unchanged source metadata and a compatible privacy notice are required. The page exposes no clear terms revision date. Kinetexa's AI use and long-term retention are not approved. The server gate is now `terms-blocked`.

## Suunto review, 6 September 2026

[API Zone](https://apizone.suunto.com/) documents free access for approved organizations serving a public audience, an application-stage agreement, development limits and separate production acceptance. Its [FAQ](https://apizone.suunto.com/faq) describes OAuth, historical workout lists, raw FIT data and workout notifications. Daily activity retrieval requires polling; the FAQ does not promise daily-data webhooks. The agreement is presented during application and has not been accepted for Kinetexa. Retention, commercial terms, branding and external AI permission therefore remain unresolved. No application has been submitted. This is a viable assessment candidate, not an approved connector.

## COROS review, 6 September 2026

The new [MCP developer guide](https://support.coros.com/hc/en-us/articles/53181619102996) explicitly lists analytics applications and permits self-service OAuth without application approval. It omits webhook push and reserves activity synchronization for the Partner API. The official [MCP repository](https://github.com/coroslab/COROS-MCP) documents activity/health tools and FIT downloads, limited to 50 files per account per calendar day. Its linked [Terms of Service](https://us.coros.com/terms), dated 23 April 2023, also restrict commercial and automated use absent permission. How the newer developer guidance resolves these restrictions for durable hosted synchronization is unclear.

[Partner API access](https://support.coros.com/hc/en-us/articles/53181766856724-Partner-API-Access) requires platform/company review and API terms acceptance. It documents raw FIT, daily health, workout push, a 1,000-call/minute limit and a three-month workout-list lookback with at most 30 days per query. Those are published limits, not verified Kinetexa capabilities. No COROS user has authorized Kinetexa and no token, backfill or sync has been exercised. [The clarification request](provider-requests.md) was explicitly approved and accepted by Resend on 6 September. Delivery and a provider reply remain unverified. External AI remains blocked for prospective connector data.
